My coffee orders

Each cup below is one coffee I bought, seen from above, in the order I bought them.

How to read the sketch

  • Position: the order I bought the drinks, left to right, then top to bottom.
  • Color of the coffee: the type of coffee, from darkest (espresso) to lightest (latte).
  • Foam ring: the milk. White is whole milk, tan is oat, pink is almond. No ring means black coffee.
  • Shape in the middle: the flavor. Circle is vanilla, triangle is caramel, square is mocha. No shape means no flavor.
  • Cup size: small, medium or large.
